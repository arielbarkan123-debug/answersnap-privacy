AnswerSnap Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 30, 2025

Overview

AnswerSnap ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we handle your information.

Information We Collect

The app accesses your device's camera and photo gallery solely to capture images of worksheets and handwriting samples for answer generation.

How We Use Your Information

Images you capture are sent to Google's Gemini API for processing to generate answers. These images are used only for this purpose and are not stored on any server.

Data Storage

We do not store your photos or any personal data. All images exist only temporarily in the app's memory during processing and are deleted when you close the app or navigate away.

Third-Party Services

We use Google's Gemini API to process images. Google's privacy policy applies to their handling of data: Google Privacy Policy

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties.

Children's Privacy

Our app does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
